## Chuna gaya approach: Quiet British Home Editorial

### Design Movement
Contemporary British hospitality editorial aur understated biophilic interiors. Yeh reference website ke clear conversion structure ko BrightNest ki softer, more considered identity mein translate karega, na ke usay copy karega.

### Core Principles
1. **Calm confidence:** Spacious layouts aur composed typography clean home ki relief ko communicate karein ge.
2. **Useful elegance:** Har visual choice booking, service comparison ya trust-building ko support kare gi.
3. **Human scale:** Staff aur homes ko staged stock imagery ki bajaye natural domestic moments ke taur par show kiya jayega.
4. **Low-friction commitment:** Booking path visible, short aur step-based hoga; lekin page ko form-heavy nahin banaya jayega.

### Color Philosophy
Warm ivory website ka breathing space hoga, jo clean linen aur natural daylight ka feel deta hai. **BrightNest Mint** (#2F9F91) primary, ownable signature colour hoga: garden-fresh magar clinical nahin. Ink navy trust aur readability create karega; muted apricot sirf navigational warmth aur micro-accents ke liye use hoga. Bright, synthetic blues ya generic purple gradients avoid kiye jayenge.

### Layout Paradigm
Ek editorial “home journal” rhythm: full-bleed visual moments, off-axis text columns, overlapping booking panel aur horizontal service strips. Hero central marketing block ke bajaye split-frame scene hoga — left mein message, right mein quiet domestic image — jis ke neeche sticky-style booking strip naturally flow kare gi.

### Signature Elements
1. **Nestline arcs:** Thin nested arch lines aur leaf-like curves jo headings, dividers aur icon containers mein subtly appear hon.
2. **Household labels:** Small all-caps service tags jo premium product packaging ki tarah reading hierarchy banate hain.
3. **Mint tile details:** Tiny mint tiles / dots as a visual cue for care, cleanliness aur motion.

### Interaction Philosophy
Buttons press par lightly compress honge aur hover par directionally slide karenge. Booking selection clear progressive disclosure use kare gi: “Service & visit”, “Your details”, “Confirm”. Har interaction informative aur calm hoga; noise ya auto-rotating content nahin hoga.

### Animation
Page entry par visual blocks 30–80ms stagger ke saath soft opacity + 12px vertical translation se appear karenge. Cards 180ms easing ke saath lift honge; large image par slow 8–12 second ambient zoom sirf reduced-motion preference ko respect karte hue. Accordion 200ms, drawer/modal 260ms aur buttons active state par scale(0.97) use karein ge. Motion kabhi booking process ko slow nahin kare gi.

### Typography System
**Display:** `DM Serif Display` for major editorial moments, using sentence case and generous line height. **Sans:** `Manrope` for interface, metadata and paragraphs. H1 responsive 54–82px, section headlines 36–52px, body 16–18px. Small labels in Manrope 700 with 0.12em tracking. Inter use nahin hoga.

### Brand Essence
**Positioning:** BrightNest Cleaning UK Birmingham households aur hosts ke liye thoughtful domestic aur specialist cleaning ko simple, considered booking experience mein deliver karta hai — local warmth ke saath.  
**Personality:** Thoughtful, reassuring, meticulous.

### Brand Voice
Headlines direct, calm aur benefit-led honge; CTAs helpful commands ki tarah sound karein ge; microcopy reassuring aur precise hogi. Generic filler bilkul nahin use hogi.

> “A lighter home starts with one well-planned clean.”
>
> “Choose a time that fits your week — we’ll handle the detail.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Ek bold, text-free nested-home symbol: two roofline arches ke andar mint leaf / sparkle form. Mark ke saath future wordmark ke liye a gently widened serif + crisp sans combination suggested hai. Header mein mark visible size par hoga aur favicon bhi isi mark se banega.

### Signature Brand Color
**BrightNest Mint — #2F9F91**

## Reference-to-original translation

Reference se conversion concepts adopt kiye jayenge: clear top navigation, service discovery, prominent booking, “how it works”, add-ons aur FAQ. BrightNest ke liye copy, visuals, hierarchy, component styling aur brand treatment completely original honge. Facebook page ke public positioning ko reflect karte hue priority services: regular, deep, end-of-tenancy, carpet, oven aur Airbnb cleaning; serving Birmingham & surrounding areas. Public page par reviews/ratings ko manufacture ya display nahin kiya jayega jab tak verified customer content available na ho.

## Style Decisions

Public copy mein build-status, implementation ya placeholder language nahin hogi; har line ek real Birmingham customer ko calmly guide kare gi. **BrightNest Mint** key actions, service numerals aur small graphic details mein signature signal rahe ga, jab ke apricot limited warmth accent ho ga. Nested-home / leaf-arc mark sirf logo tak limited nahin rahe ga: nestline arcs ko hero labels, service-card artwork aur brand containers mein repeat kiya jayega taake page ki visual language ownable aur consistently BrightNest lage.

### Accepted review amendments
- Customer-facing dashboard states must remain calm and helpful; they must not expose API, deployment, environment-variable, or build language.
- The supplied BrightNest mark should be reused consistently in customer-facing account surfaces rather than substituted with generic iconography.
- Trust proof must remain grounded in authentic client material; where source context is not available, prefer service standards and process reassurance over invented reviews.
