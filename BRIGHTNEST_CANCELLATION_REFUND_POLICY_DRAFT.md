## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy explains how customers may cancel or request a change to a BrightNest Cleaning UK booking, how BrightNest may handle late cancellations and missed appointments, and when refunds or credits may be available. It applies to domestic and specialist cleaning services booked through the BrightNest website, email, telephone, or another agreed booking channel.

This policy should be read together with BrightNest’s Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, booking confirmation, and any written quote or service-specific terms. If there is a conflict, mandatory consumer rights apply first and the specific written booking terms apply only to the extent they are fair, transparent, and lawful.

## 2. Booking confirmation and when a booking is formed

A booking becomes confirmed only when BrightNest has accepted the request and sent written confirmation, or when the customer has otherwise accepted a quote and BrightNest has agreed to reserve the appointment. A request submitted through the website is not necessarily an accepted booking until BrightNest confirms it.

The booking confirmation should identify the service, scheduled date and time, service address or postcode, quoted price or pricing basis, payment or deposit requirement, and any specific cancellation terms that apply. Customers should keep the confirmation email for their records.

## 3. How to cancel or request a reschedule

Customers should submit a cancellation or reschedule request as soon as they know their plans have changed. Requests may be made through the secure customer dashboard, by email at **[insert BrightNest customer email]**, or by telephone at **[insert telephone number]**.

A request should include the customer’s name, booking reference, scheduled date, and the requested action. A cancellation or reschedule is not complete until BrightNest acknowledges it in writing. The time BrightNest receives the request will be used when applying any notice period.

Customers should not rely solely on a message left with a cleaner or an unconfirmed social-media message. BrightNest will make reasonable efforts to respond promptly and will not unreasonably delay acknowledgement.

## 4. Proposed standard cancellation window

**Proposed BrightNest rule — confirm before publication:** Customers may cancel or request one reschedule without a cancellation charge when BrightNest receives the request at least **48 hours before the scheduled start time**.

Where a customer requests a reschedule within this notice period, BrightNest will try to offer a suitable alternative date and time. A new appointment is subject to availability and may require a revised quote if the service scope, duration, location, or frequency changes.

BrightNest should consider offering a longer notice period for recurring customers or high-value specialist bookings where the appointment requires more preparation. Any different period must be clearly stated in the booking confirmation.

## 5. Late cancellations and short-notice rescheduling

A late cancellation is a cancellation received less than the proposed 48-hour notice period before the scheduled start time. A short-notice reschedule is treated in the same way if BrightNest cannot reasonably fill the original appointment slot.

**Proposed charge structure — confirm amount and wording before publication:** BrightNest may charge the lesser of:

1. the reasonable direct loss caused by the late cancellation, including staff time or other non-recoverable costs already committed to the booking; or
2. **[insert fixed amount or percentage, if BrightNest chooses to use one]**, provided that the amount is a fair and transparent estimate of likely direct loss and is not an automatic penalty.

BrightNest will take reasonable steps to reduce its loss, including attempting to reallocate staff or rebook the appointment slot. If the slot is successfully rebooked, BrightNest should review whether a charge remains justified and reduce or waive it where appropriate.

Any charge must be explained to the customer on request. BrightNest should not retain more of a deposit or advance payment than is reasonably needed to cover the loss caused by the cancellation.

## 6. Same-day cancellation and missed appointments

A same-day cancellation is a cancellation received on the scheduled date. A missed appointment or no-show occurs where the cleaner cannot reasonably access the property at the agreed time because the customer is unavailable, access instructions are missing or incorrect, or the property cannot safely be entered.

**Proposed treatment — confirm before publication:** BrightNest may apply a reasonable charge for a same-day cancellation or missed appointment when staff have attended, travel has been undertaken, or the appointment time could not reasonably be rebooked. The charge should reflect the direct loss and any work actually performed, rather than operate as a punitive fee.

BrightNest will first try to contact the customer using the booking contact details. If access is not possible, the cleaner should wait for **[insert reasonable waiting period]** where practical and safe, record the attempted attendance, and notify BrightNest. No cleaner is required to wait in an unsafe situation.

No missed-appointment charge should apply where the failure resulted from BrightNest’s error, a serious safety issue that BrightNest caused, or circumstances outside the customer’s reasonable control, subject to the facts of the case.

## 7. Cooling-off and other statutory rights

For many services arranged online, by telephone, or by mail order, consumers generally have a 14-day cooling-off period under the Consumer Contracts Regulations. The exact application depends on how and when the contract was formed and whether an exception applies. A customer who asks BrightNest to start providing the service during that period may be responsible for the proportion of service supplied up to cancellation. These rights must be explained clearly and must not be removed by this policy. [1] [2] [3]

If a customer asks for a cleaning visit to take place during the cooling-off period, BrightNest should obtain the customer’s clear request to begin the service before the period ends and should provide the required cancellation information. BrightNest should obtain professional advice on the exact wording and process before relying on this exception.

Nothing in this policy limits rights relating to services that are not provided with reasonable care and skill, are not as described, or otherwise fail to meet applicable consumer law. A cancellation charge must be fair, reasonable, transparent, and connected to BrightNest’s genuine direct loss. [1] [4]

## 8. Refund eligibility

A refund may be available in the following circumstances:

| Situation | Proposed refund treatment |
| --- | --- |
| BrightNest cancels and cannot offer a suitable replacement | Refund amounts paid for the unprovided service, normally to the original payment method. |
| Customer cancels within an applicable statutory cooling-off period | Refund amounts required by law, subject to any lawful deduction for service the customer expressly asked BrightNest to provide during that period. |
| Customer cancels within the agreed notice period | Refund the amount paid for the cancelled visit, or offer a customer-approved credit or reschedule. |
| BrightNest accepts a late cancellation without charge | Refund or credit according to the written confirmation BrightNest sends for that case. |
| BrightNest has performed part of a service and the customer is entitled to a remedy | Refund or price reduction may be available for the affected part, depending on the circumstances and applicable law. |
| Customer cancels late or misses an appointment | Refund may be reduced by a fair, reasonable amount reflecting BrightNest’s direct loss and any work or travel already undertaken. |
| Customer is dissatisfied with service quality | Customer should contact BrightNest promptly so BrightNest can investigate, offer a reasonable remedy, and determine whether a refund or price reduction is appropriate. |

A refund will not automatically be due merely because a customer changes their mind outside an applicable statutory right or agreed cancellation window. However, BrightNest may choose to offer a goodwill refund or credit on a case-by-case basis.

BrightNest should not describe a deposit as “non-refundable” without explaining the lawful basis and the circumstances in which it may be retained. Any retained amount should be proportionate and should not exceed the loss reasonably caused by the cancellation.

## 9. How refunds are processed

Approved refunds will normally be sent to the original payment method. BrightNest should process an approved refund within **[insert business-day target, such as 5 business days]** after approval. The time taken for the money to appear may depend on the payment provider or bank.

## 10. BrightNest cancellation or service disruption

BrightNest may need to cancel, delay, or change a visit because of cleaner illness, severe weather, transport disruption, property access problems, unsafe working conditions, equipment failure, or another circumstance that makes the service impractical or unsafe.

BrightNest will notify the customer as soon as reasonably possible and will offer an alternative appointment where available. If the customer does not accept the alternative, BrightNest will refund amounts paid for the part of the service that will not be provided, subject to any lawful deduction for services already supplied.

BrightNest will not charge a customer for a cancellation caused solely by BrightNest. If BrightNest has caused additional reasonably foreseeable loss, the customer should contact BrightNest so the matter can be reviewed under the applicable contract and law.

## 11. Cleaning quality concerns and remedies

A cancellation request is not the only route for a service concern. If a customer believes a cleaning service was incomplete or did not meet the agreed description, they should contact BrightNest within **[insert practical reporting period, such as 48 hours]**, preferably with photographs and the booking reference.

## 12. Exceptional circumstances

BrightNest will consider reasonable requests relating to serious illness, bereavement, hospital treatment, household emergencies, severe travel disruption, or another circumstance that the customer could not reasonably avoid. Supporting information should only be requested where genuinely necessary and proportionate.

Exceptional-circumstance discretion does not guarantee a refund, but BrightNest will consider whether a charge can be waived, reduced, transferred to a new date, or replaced with a credit. BrightNest will apply this discretion consistently and will record the decision against the booking.
