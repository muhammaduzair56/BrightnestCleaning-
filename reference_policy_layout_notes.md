# Reference PDF layout notes

The supplied Martilla policy is a two-page portrait client policy. Page one uses a strong full-width dark blue header with the company name and a right-aligned title, followed by a short introduction and four numbered sections separated by thin rules. Each section has a bold blue heading with a vertical accent bar and concise body paragraphs, plus a light bordered callout for a critical safety/payment note. Page two is intentionally sparse: a pale blue promotional/information callout near the top, a divider, a centered closing note, and a small footer with company website and page number.

For BrightNest, retain the useful structure—brand header, concise introduction, numbered policy sections, one highlighted callout, airy second page, and page footer—but do not copy Martilla’s logo, blue palette, wording, payment claims, or testimonial/community content. Adapt it to BrightNest’s Quiet British Home Editorial system: ivory paper, ink navy header/type, mint accents, refined serif display headings, soft pale-green callout, BrightNest logo, and the already drafted cancellation/refund policy. Because BrightNest’s legal business details and VAT status are unconfirmed, the document should remain a policy draft/service policy rather than claim official VAT status.
